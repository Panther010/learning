ETL vs ELT: the choice of where to do the heavy lifting is not a trend, it's a compliance and cost lever.

ETL follows Extract  to  Transform  to  Load.
ELT follows Extract  to  Load  to  Transform.

In an e-commerce stack, PostgreSQL feeds Orders, Salesforce feeds Customers, APIs feed Payments, and JSON feeds Clickstream.

- With ETL, we clean, join and apply business rules before loading the warehouse.
- With ELT, we first land the raw data in a lakehouse and then use SQL, dbt or Spark to build staging, intermediate and business models.

- Early transformation (ETL) keeps the warehouse lean and satisfies source-system constraints or security rules that must be enforced before data arrives.
- Raw landing (ELT) enables audit trails, re-processing, and multiple downstream models without re-extracting data.
- ELT can incur higher compute costs because the warehouse must run the transformation workload.
- ETL may add latency because transformations happen outside the warehouse, but it can reduce the load on the target system.

Rule of thumb: choose ELT when you need to preserve the raw layer for audit, re-processing or multiple models; choose ETL when source constraints or compliance require transformation before load.
