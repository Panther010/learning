Batch vs Streaming vs Micro-batch
𝗟𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗻𝗰𝘆‑𝗗𝗿𝗶𝘃𝗲𝗻 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗹𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗮 𝘁𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗻𝗼𝗹𝗼𝗴𝘆 𝗰𝗵𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲, 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝘆'𝗿𝗲 𝗮 𝗯𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻.
When you ask, "Should we batch, micro‑batch or stream?" the answer comes from the cost of delay, not from the stack's capabilities.

The three models form a ladder:

• 𝗕𝗮𝘁𝗰𝗵 - Collect → Process → Publish
• Typical windows: hourly or daily
• Use cases: daily revenue reports, large historical ETL, backfills
• Strengths: high throughput, low operational cost, predictable timing
• Weaknesses: high latency, no real‑time insight

• 𝗠𝗶𝗰𝗿𝗼‑𝗯𝗮𝘁𝗰𝗵 - Collect a small window → Process → Publish
• Typical windows: 10-30 seconds
• Use cases: dashboards that refresh every 30 s, incremental loads, click‑stream aggregation
• Strengths: near‑real‑time, moderate complexity, easier to scale than full streaming
• Weaknesses: still a batch cycle, ordering and state are less strict than streaming

• 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗲𝗮𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 - Event arrives → Process → Output
• Typical windows: milliseconds to seconds
• Use cases: fraud detection, real‑time alerts, IoT telemetry, recommendation engines
• Strengths: true real‑time, immediate action possible
• Weaknesses: ordering guarantees, late‑arrival handling, duplicate suppression, state management, checkpointing, higher operational overhead

𝗥𝗲𝗮𝗹‑𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗹𝗱 𝗲𝘅𝗮𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗲
An e‑commerce platform can layer all three:

• Batch: daily revenue reporting to finance
• Micro‑batch: sales dashboard that updates every 30 s for marketing
• Streaming: fraud detection on every payment event

The same business can decide to keep the fraud engine in streaming because a 30‑minute delay would render the decision useless, while the sales dashboard can tolerate a few seconds of lag.

𝗧𝗿𝗮𝗱𝗲‑𝗼𝗳𝗳𝘀

• Latency vs. complexity - the lower the latency you need, the more complex the pipeline becomes.
• Cost of delay - a 30‑minute lag on a monthly report is acceptable; the same lag on a fraud decision is not.
• Operational overhead - streaming requires robust checkpointing, state recovery, and strict delivery semantics.

𝗥𝘂𝗹𝗲 𝗼𝗳 𝘁𝗵𝘂𝗺𝗯

• Hours/days → Batch
• Seconds/minutes → Micro‑batch
• Milliseconds/seconds + immediate action → Streaming

Choose the tier that matches the business's tolerance for delay. Don't add real‑time complexity just because the technology allows it.
